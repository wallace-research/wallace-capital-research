Welcome and thank you for visiting my website

Equity research, DCF models, and forward projections

This site is a personal capital research project by me, Ty Wallace, an aspiring finance student. It serves as a place to store my equity writeups for an array of companies chosen for diversity of various sectors and brand-name recognition, discounted cash flow models with independent WACC calculation and other intrinsic assumptions, forward projection models (share price forecasts), stock comparisons to major indices, and economic essays (also bridging my love of philosophy). My intention is to combine my passion for writing with finance, as the prose is meant to be informative, insightful, and fun, rather than strictly rigid and fully data-driven.

Please be aware that all financial information is drawn from publicly available data and free tools. Nothing on this site is meant to encompass professional investment advice as this is a self-taught finance project.

What to expect and how to navigate the website

  • Welcome — this overview/home page
  • Scorecard — a simple table to track my recommendations vs. subsequent real-world returns
  • Writings — longer-form economic and market essays
  • Company pages/tabs — full equity reports, investment summary tables, and model downloads for each company
