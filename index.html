Welcome

Equity research, DCF models, and forward projections

This site is a personal capital research project by Ty Wallace. It collects independent equity write-ups, discounted cash flow (DCF) models, and forward projection work across a focused set of public companies. The goal is thoughtful, structured analysis—not professional investment advice.

All financial highlights and insights are drawn from publicly available data and free tools. Nothing on this site constitutes professional investment advice. Do your own research.

What you’ll find here

  • Welcome — this overview
  • Scorecard — a simple table to track recommendations vs. subsequent returns
  • Writings — longer-form economic and market essays
  • Company pages — full equity reports, investment summary tables, and model downloads for each name
